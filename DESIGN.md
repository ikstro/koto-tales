# Льошині казки — design rules

The source of the look is the game's own world, the «Діорама» skin: warm golden-hour toy bricks with rounded bevels and studs. The interface is made of the same material. Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-28 (comp `design-lab/2026-10-28-ui/comps/03-pieces`).

## Principles
- **The UI is toy pieces from this world**, rendered with the skin's plastic and the same sun (upper left), each with its own soft shadow. Never: white web circles, dark-outlined cartoon buttons, wooden boards, glossy candy gradients, stars, red badges.
- **The hero is always visible**: controls hug the corners, the centre stays free. Tree crowns stay whole (no stumps): only leaf blocks between the camera and the hero that cover the hero on screen open, a round window ~3.6 blocks across around the hero's spot, whole blocks with a dotted rim. Everything else tall dissolves only on the narrow camera–hero line (`src/render/skins/cutaway.ts`). See «Camera».
- **Nothing needs reading**: icons are printed on tiles; the only text is numbers (coins, monsters defeated, album counts). Finds are announced by voice only — no pop-up cards over the game. The one exception is «Зміст гри» in the pause menu, a page for the parents (see «Contents»); the child needs none of it.
- **One touch = one thing**, answered at once by motion and a click.
- **Sized by the screen, not the mock-up**: one unit `--u: clamp(44px, 8.5vmin, 76px)`; the bow ≈ 1.35u, other actions 1u, top pieces ≈ 0.8u. Nothing overlaps at 1280×800, 1024×600, 844×390 or 390×844 (portrait phone uses a compact layout).
- **Low, out of the way** (the owner's call on 2026-10-29, replacing the tablet ⅔-height rule): on every screen the joystick sits in the bottom-left corner and the actions at the bottom-right edge, so the controls never cover the game. The actions stand in one column hugging the right edge: the kit box on top, the lightning, the bow at the bottom by the thumb (the box and the lightning swapped on the owner's call, 2026-10-29). Just left of the column: the **jump** by the bow (always there); a prompt (moon, fold, chest) by the lightning; by the box the small ✕ while placing, otherwise the note / food / fly / remote, the pot at her stove, and the weather piece beside Юля.
- **The tale piece** left of the minimap: the tale step's picture on an 8-stud ring (how far it has come), the asker's face in its corner (Пупся or the race's elder); a tap says the ask again. Hidden while no tale is under way, while sleeping and while the boss bar is up.
- **Top right**: one hero piece (the current face; a tap fans out the four heroes), album, fullscreen, pause. Pause opens a plate of picture pieces: continue, sound, «?» (the guide), «Зміст гри» (a list printed on a sky-blue tile, see «Contents»), the door (exit: saved, Пупся says goodbye, the installed game closes; in a browser tab it goes back to the title), and «Почати знову» (a sprout on its own stud ring: held, never tapped — the ring lights over 2 s, then a new story on the same world starts at the title, keeping only sound and difficulty; the old save is kept aside in storage; a short tap wiggles it and Пупся says to hold); difficulty as three pieces with 1/2/3 skulls.

## Heroes and weapons
Four heroes (the owner's call, 2026-10-30): Льоша, Соломія, Пупся and Давид, Льоша's friend (very short light-brown hair, a big smile, a lemon-yellow tee, jeans, a toy sword always in his right hand). Any of them can be woken and played; the others follow (two beside, the third behind).
- **Everyone shoots far, and the same**: one button, one strength (`HERO_STATS`), each hero with their own weapon and its own whoosh — Льоша's bow (arrows stick in walls), Соломія's wand (spinning pink stars with a sparkle trail), Пупся the battle mage's cat magic (glowing violet purr-balls with a cream paw print, she casts them), Давид's throwing axes (spinning end over end). Stars, purr-balls and axes puff in their colour on a wall. Armour (`bolt`, `lantern`) stops every weapon alike.
- **The lightning is the child's**: only the lightning button casts it; helpers only shoot.

## Camera
The owner's calls on 2026-10-29: the hero is not made smaller, and there is no zoom.
- One fixed view that never turns (up on the joystick = up on the screen). No zoom by hand or by itself; only the pull-back near the land-eater stays, and the one in the house's flight (see «Бабуся», «The flight»).
- Steep: it looks down at ~60° along the line to the hero, and the hero stands a little below the middle of the screen (chest at ~58% of the height), so tall things (a lighthouse, a castle) and the way ahead fit above. The hero keeps the size on screen they had before (~85 px tall at 1280×800). `FRAMINGS` in `src/core/camera.ts`; `?cam=<n>` shows the others for comparing shots (`design-lab/camera/`).
- **The mark**: the hero the child plays has a slim gold ring (`#ffd23a`, the skin's plastic) lying on the ground under the feet with a soft gold glow along it, breathing gently. Helpers have none. On a switch it pops up under the new hero with a little hop (the hero selection spring). While jumping it stays on the ground; in water it floats on the surface (`src/render/heroMark.ts`).

## Pieces
- Round printed tile (fire — the current hero's weapon: Льоша's bow, Соломія's star wand, Пупся's paw print, Давид's axe —, lightning, jump — a green tile with a spring and an arrow up, ✓, moon, fold, note, door), heart tile, the game's coin, mustard number tile, round hero tile with the rendered face (active: lifted, gold rim; others keep full colour), book piece (album), round joystick plate with a stud knob, the kit box (a mustard toolbox with a coral lid) on its own stud ring, the kit's buildings as their real models, the lantern as a real toy lantern piece (dark frame and cap, ring handle, warm glass, bright core, a soft glow behind it); the boss bar's lantern slots are small ones.

## Screens
- **Start**: «Розбуди героїв» — no words; the four heroes asleep in the bedroom, the touched one wakes and becomes the player (`src/ui/wake.ts`); a few seconds later the prologue begins (see «Tales»).

## Kit
No digging and no loose bricks (the owner's call): the ground is never changed. Achievements open **ready-made buildings** in a kit box; the child puts them up anywhere and packs them away again.
- **The box** sits at the top of the action column on a stud ring. A tap opens the kit plate: each building as its model with how many are left (none left: faded); the last cell is the next reward as a dark silhouette with its task's picture (Зомбік, coin, cloud, moon, boss) breathing on it — a tap says the task.
- **Placing**: a building chosen in the plate walks in front of the hero as a breathing see-through ghost, always on the nearest free spot (never on trees, water, the house, its doorstep and campfire, the portal arch or another building; the floor within two blocks of the hero's feet). The box turns into a green ✓ with a small coral ✕; ✓ drops the building in from above with a bounce. Ground uneven by one block gets a teal plinth. Buildings face the camera, like toys set out on a table.
- **Folding**: next to one of the child's buildings a fold piece (arrow into a box) breathes beside the actions; a tap packs it back into the box.
- **Tasks** (`src/content/progress.ts`): one at a time, in a circle — monsters, coins, new places on the map, monsters, a night's sleep, the boss — each circle asking a little more, up to a cap. Once the windmill is out, the second «monsters» of a circle becomes «light a lighthouse»; once the dance floor is out, every other circle's «coins» becomes «dance» (seconds danced, near music or on the floor). The ring's studs show how far; when full: a fanfare, «Ура!», the reward's line. Rewards are cards in order — the portal; the windmill with four poles; three lamps; the solar panel; the battery; the engine-generator; the big speaker; the dance floor; the traffic light; the disco ball; the tesla tower; the DJ console; the radar; the Wi-Fi antenna; two transmitters; the picnic; the ice-cream kiosk; the swings; the power station — then, endlessly and by turns, more of a building (each in turn, up to a cap; poles and transmitters two at a time) and an **upgrade**, then coins; these endless ones come home as parcels in the postbox (see «Home life»).
- **Upgrades** (`src/content/upgrades.ts`): a reward raises a building the child has by a level, up to 3; every one standing gets better at once, and its cell in the kit plate shows the level as gold studs (no numbers). Generator +2 sparks, tesla strikes 0.25 s sooner, radar +16 sweep, big speaker heard +5 and danced to +2 further (gold at level 3), Wi-Fi zone +4, power station +5 sparks. Each says its line («Генератор став сильнішим!»).
- The plate shows only what the child has (in the box or standing); with many buildings it grows wider before taller.
- **Tabs** (from 13 buildings; `KIT_TABS`, each card's `tab`): a row of round picture tabs at the top of the plate — home (the tent), music (the boombox), power (the tesla tower), nature (a daisy), fun (the swings). The active one is lifted with a gold rim, the row stays put while the plate grows under it, a tap on a tab gives a click and keeps the choice when the plate is closed and opened. Cells shrink rather than scroll. On portrait screens the plate opens above the action column.
- **What the party carries** (the owner's call, 2026-10-30): the box holds everything the party carries. Items that can be used (`use` on the item card, CONTENT.md) sit in a **second row** of the kit plate, under the buildings, each as its album sticker tile with how many on a number tile. A tap uses one: the plate closes, the effect starts at once with a ring of sparkles in its colour round the party (`src/systems/effects.ts`). None carried: no row, the plate is as before. Keys (`use: 'auto'`) never show there; they fire by themselves next to their lock. The prompt pieces (apple, cone) stay as they are.
- Buildings: **tent** (sleep in it at night), **boombox** (music), **lantern** (never runs out; inside the boss's wasteland the box picks it by itself and ✓ pulses), the power ones below, the villages' prizes (see «Villages»), food and the swings (see «Food and swings»), the garden bed (see «Home life»).

## Power
One rule: **what stands close (7 blocks) joins by itself** — wires sag between neighbours, sparks run along the ones that work. Nobody drags a wire. Numbers are «sparks» a second (`src/content/power.ts`, tested):
| Building | Power | Look |
|---|---|---|
| windmill | +2 always | tall mast, spinning coral blades |
| solar panel | +3 × daylight | blue panel turning to the sun; rests at night |
| battery | stores 200, gives back what is missing | big toy battery, five studs show the charge |
| engine-generator | +6 always | engine, flywheel, meshing gears, puffs of smoke; a quiet putt nearby |
| pole | 0, carries it on | wooden pole with a crossarm |
| street lamp | −1; at night no monster appears within 8 | teal pole, warm lamp head with a halo and a pool |
| lighthouse (in the world) | −2; within 16 | see «World» |
| big speaker | −2 | tall cabinet, thumping cones, dust hopping on top (see «Music») |
| dance floor | −2 | 3×3 tiles lighting on the beat, brightest under feet |
| disco ball | −1 | mirror ball on a stand; coloured spots circle the ground at night |
| DJ console | −1 | turntables, sliders, a display in its song's colour; plays music itself (see «Music») |
| tesla tower | −3 | copper coil, a ball crackling; strikes the nearest monster within 7 every 1.3 s |
| power station (the last listed reward) | +20 always; joins everything within 24 (and 12 up or down) | a big toy station: dome, copper coils, a steaming chimney; its links are crackling arcs instead of wires |
| ice-cream kiosk | −1 | see «Food and swings» |
| traffic light | −1 | green 6 s, yellow 1.5 s, red 6 s (all lights together); on red, monsters within 9 stop and wait |
| garland (elves) | −1 | bulbs on a string between two posts, blinking on the beat; at night no monster appears within 6 |
| carousel (gnomes) | −2 | turning platform with animal seats under a striped canopy |
| searchlight (robots) | −2 | a lamp head sweeping its beam; at night a monster caught in it (14) freezes for 3 s |
| village heart | −2 | the race's machine in the middle of a village (see «Villages») |
- Every powered building has a little bulb: warm = works, blinking coral = the network is short, grey = joined to nothing.
- Four times a second each network is balanced: enough → everything works, the spare fills batteries; short → batteries give; still short → **the whole network blinks and nothing on it works** (lamps don't protect), and Пупся says «струму мало» (at most once a minute, only nearby; at night with only the sun she says the panel is asleep). The first network that lights: «Ура, є струм!».
- The tesla never strikes a dancing monster (music means peace); waiting monsters at a red light neither chase nor bite.
- So it teaches itself: windmill + lighthouse = enough; windmill + three lamps = blinking; sun + lamp = fine by day, blinking at night until a battery comes.
- While a powered building's ghost walks along, a see-through wire shows what it would join.

## World: rivers, bridges, lighthouses
No sea (the owner's call): lakes and winding rivers, 5–6 blocks wide with sandy banks, cutting little valleys through the hills; the yard at home stays dry and no river comes within ~44 blocks of it. Everything comes from the seed (`src/world/terrain.ts`, `src/world/landmarks.ts`); the child never changes it.
- **Bridges**: about one every 56 blocks of river — toy plank bridges with teal railings, slightly arched. Their flat deck is invisible solid cells, so everyone walks over (heroes can also swim).
- **Lighthouses**: at most one per 128×128 region, on flat dry shore where there is plenty of water (a lake or a wide bend), never on trees; the nearest is ~85 blocks from home. A striped cream/coral tower ~10 high, a teal balcony, a glass lamp room, a mustard dome; a little coral boat bobs in front. Its tower dissolves on the camera–hero line, like trees.
- A lighthouse is dark until a network with enough power reaches it (bring the windmill and set it beside). Then its lamp burns, two warm beams sweep over the water (stronger at night), monsters do not appear within 16 at night. The first time: a fanfare, «Маяк світить!», a lighthouse sticker in the album (×N lit). Without power it goes dark again, but it stays counted.
- The first dark lighthouse met: Пупся «Ой, маяк! Він темний…».
- Map: dark lighthouses appear once their place is seen, lit ones always; the nearest lit one (or, during the lighthouse task, the nearest dark one) has the rim arrow.

## Music
- Four little songs made in code, and twelve more on Бабуся's discs (polka, waltz, twist, `src/voice/music.ts`; гопак, reggae, march, space, robo-funk, fish blues, the purr beat made of Пупся's purring, carol, her own song, `src/voice/discTunes.ts`; no files, always tunes of our own): **disco** (kick, clap, hats, octave bass, chord stabs, a bell tune; C–G–Am–F, 120 a minute), **rock** (kick and snare, chugging power chords, a square riff; 132), **happy** (oom-pah bass, plucked chords, a whistled tune, shaker and woodblock; 108), **lullaby** (a music box over a soft pad, no drums; 72). It gets louder as the hero comes closer, dips under the voice and stops while paused.
- **Every speaker its own song, the bigger the louder** (the owner's call, 2026-10-31; `src/content/music.ts`, tested): the boombox plays disco, quietest (no power; heard within 18, monsters dance within 9); the DJ console plays the song its note picked (a new game starts it on the happy song), in the middle (power; heard within 24, dance within 11); the big speaker plays rock, loudest (power; heard within 30, dance within 14).
- One song is heard at a time, never two mixed: the speaker the hero stands at, and further off the one that sounds strongest (bigger and closer); walking from one to another, one fades out as the other comes in. The dancing and the lights follow the beat of the song heard.
- **Monsters near playing music dance and do not bite**; helpers leave dancing monsters alone. The land-eater dances too, but only for 6 s (no stomp, no bite, mouth shut — a breather, not a win), then shakes it off with a roar and won't dance again for 30 s; Пупся laughs the first time in a fight («Ха! Пожирач танцює!»). Standing still near the music, the party dances too; on a working dance floor at once; a step and they stop. The child's dancing time is counted (the «dance» task).
- **DJ console**: next to a working one a note piece breathes beside the bow; a tap plays the console's next song (the speakers keep theirs), Пупся names it («Диско!», «Рок!», «Весела пісенька!», «Колискова… Тсс»). The song is saved; the console's display glows in its colour. The note steps only through songs the party has: the four built in and every disc found.
- **Jukebox** (Бабуся's gift, no power; heard within 20, dance within 10): the DJ console's note and song, without the wires.
- The boombox thumps on the beat, its display changes colour, notes float up.

## Portals
- The kit's first reward. An arch (teal pillars, mustard keystone, a turning warm swirl) stands in the front yard, left of the campfire.
- Far from home, a tap on the house on the big map opens a portal where the party stands and carries them out in front of the arch; that field portal stays, with its swirl mark on the map. Walking into the arch takes them back to it; walking into it takes them home. One field portal: the next jump home moves it. Before the reward, the tap gets Пупся's «скоро в нас буде портал».

## Home
The heroes' house (the owner's call, 2026-10-30: more room for four, and a reason to come back): 13×9 blocks, log corners, plank walls, glass windows, a cobble rim, the stepped tile roof that fades while a hero is inside, the chimney over the stove; the door in the middle of the front, the campfire and the portal arch in the yard (`src/content/house.ts` blueprint and `HOME` spots, models `src/assets/home.ts`, run by `src/systems/house.ts`). Only the shell, the floor and the lantern are blocks; the furniture is props.
- **Bedroom** (left): a bunk bed — Давид below (green), Льоша on top (blue) with a ladder —, Соломія's bed (pink), the lantern between them, a red rug with Пупся's round cushion.
- **Kitchen** (right, on a checked floor): a teal counter with a sink under the window, jars on a shelf, a stove with a pot that steams (more while eating) and a glowing oven, a pipe up to the chimney, a fridge with magnets and a drawing; a table with a checked cloth, a plate of pies and four chairs (Пупся's at the end, with a cushion). It is a **food place** like the picnic: inside the house near the table, while someone is hurt, the apple piece breathes; a tap — the party sits down and eats warm pies for 3 s (Пупся a fish), every heart full, «Смачно!», and 20 s of fast running as after ice cream. **A tap on the table itself** (the owner's call, 2026-10-31) does the same whenever the hero is in the house, hurt or not — the game's first tap on a thing in the world (`src/ui/worldTap.ts`: a short, still touch anywhere off the pieces, the joystick's half too). Standing still at one of its chairs, the hero sits down on it, as on the swings; a step gets up.
- **Treasure cabinet** («полиця скарбів») on the back wall, the chest in front of it: rows of little teal plinths, one per artifact (at least eight). A found artifact stands on its plinth, turning slowly over a gold glow; the rest wait empty and dark.
- **Aquarium** by Пупся's cushion: four toy fish swimming loops, a plant, a rock, bubbles. A tap sprinkles orange flakes from the top and the fish rush up for 10 s; the first feeding a day gives 2 coins by the tank, and Пупся comments, a cat watching fish (at most every 30 s).
- The house lamp (the one real light) hangs in the middle of the room. An old save's small house is pulled down and the big one put up around the same centre (`save.houseV`); kit buildings it now covers go back into the box.

### Home life
Reasons to come back home (the owner's calls, 2026-11-07). The save keeps it in `save.home` (`src/content/home.ts`: pets, garden, post).
- **Сірко at his kennel** (`src/systems/pets.ts`, rules `src/content/pets.ts`): a coral bowl in front of the kennel door. Each morning he is hungry: while the party is within ~12 he sits by the bowl wagging, the bowl breathes, he whines now and then, and Пупся says so once a day. A tap on the bowl or on him gives him one thing the party carries, dog-safe only, in this order: Бабуся's pie, egg, carrot, apple; once a day. He eats, runs off 6–10 blocks, sniffs, runs back and brings 3–5 coins (55 %) or a kitchen product. Nothing he eats carried: he wags and Пупся says what he likes. Never fed: nothing happens.
- **The garden bed** (kit building `garden`, tab home, 3×2, no power; every kit has one, old saves get one; `src/systems/garden.ts`, rules `src/content/garden.ts`): a wooden raised bed with six spots, all growing one crop — potato, carrot, cherry or apple. One tap does the next sensible thing: pick a ripe crop (3 into the box), plant the first carried one (1 taken), water it (the bed's own little can pours over the rows), or a wiggle and a word from Пупся. Each new day adds a stage, a watered or rainy day one more: sprout, growing, ripe, so a bed watered on planting day is ripe the next morning. A ripe bed breathes when the party is near, and Пупся says so once a day; folded back, it returns its seed, or the harvest if ripe. Nothing withers.
- **The postbox** (`src/systems/mail.ts`, rules `src/content/post.ts`, model `src/assets/postbox.ts`): a blue toy postbox on a post right of the door. While something waits, its coral flag springs up, a letter peeks from the slot and the box breathes. A tap or walking up opens one thing, oldest first: a letter's plane hops out and the tale begins; a parcel or a gift hops out with confetti. An empty box: the door wiggles, and now and then Пупся says what the flag means. What comes there: tale letters (see «Tales»), the kit box's endless rewards as **parcels** (the fanfare at once, the reward's line on opening; parcels count towards the caps), and guests' gifts.
- **Guests at the campfire** (`src/systems/guests.ts`, rules `src/content/guests.ts`): two toy logs by the fire. Standing still at one, the hero sits down and the party with them; a step gets up. From evening, every second day at most, while they sit and no monster is coming at them, a guest comes: the elder of a village they have met or the grandmother who is away (once «Я приїхала!» is done), in turn; the one at home never. A puff at the yard's edge (the race's colour, or petals), they walk up, sit on the other log, wave, say hello and one bit of their life, give 5 coins or a kitchen product (by turns), say goodbye and vanish in a puff; Пупся greets them («У нас гості!»). If the party leaves before the gift, it waits in the postbox.

## Sleep
- From evening till dawn, inside the house, next to a tent or at Бабуся's, a moon piece breathes beside the actions (and Пупся says so once a night). A tap: everyone lies down (the bunk bed, Соломія's bed and the cushion at home, side by side in the tent), «На добраніч», a dark fade with warm stars, morning — hearts full, a new day, saved.

## Map
- **Minimap** (always on, `src/ui/map.ts`): a round studded plate ≈ 1.7u under the top-right row (left of the row on short landscape screens). The map is printed on the plate's tilted top: north up, the hero in the middle, ~24 blocks around. Redrawn 5×/s on a 2D canvas.
- **Big map**: a tap on the minimap opens it on a studded plate and pauses the game; coral ✕ or a tap outside closes it. Everything explored is fitted around the hero; when it no longer fits at a readable size, drag to look around.
- **Explored = seen**: the chunk (16×16) the hero stands in and its neighbours; the 5×5 chunks around the house are known from the start. The rest is under soft cream cloud puffs, never a flat sheet. Saved as a short list of `"cx,cz"`.
- **Tiles**: one block = a 3×3 px brick from above (lit upper-left edge, shaded lower-right, a stud highlight in the middle) in the «Діорама» colours: olive grass mosaic, dark-green / olive / golden crowns, golden sand, teal water, warm snow, sandstone; relief shading with the sun from the upper left.
- **Marks are pieces**: the hero's face tile, helpers as small tiles in their colours, monsters as purple Зомбік tiles, the house as a little house piece, the field portal as a small swirl tile, lighthouses as little striped towers (dark or lit), villages as the elder's face on the race's colour (once seen; a tap jumps there through the portal), Бабуся's house as her face (always known; a tap jumps to her, see «Бабуся»). A mark may act on a tap (the house: the portal home). Marks set with `setMapMarker` (house, boss, quest places) with `pointer` sit on the minimap rim when far away, with a coral arrow on the plate edge pointing the way; `zoneRadius` draws a dashed coral danger ring, visible over the clouds.

## Villages
Four villages, one per race, from the seed (`src/world/villages.ts`): elves in a forest (~140 blocks from home), gnomes in the hills (~190), robots on a meadow (~245), aliens in a sandy crater on a far meadow (~335). The world flattens a 27×27 square with soft edges, no trees, paved in the race's way, the gate facing home.
- **The square** (`LAYOUT` in `src/content/villages.ts`, models `src/assets/villages.ts`): the race's heart machine in the middle (elves a lantern tree, gnomes a forge with a smoking chimney, robots a charging mast with a blinking beacon, aliens a dish whose beam rises into the sky once powered; each seen from afar), four houses with doors to the plaza, the guest house at the back, the arch at the gate, a portal by the path (always facing the camera), lamps.
- **Folk** (`src/assets/parts/{elf,gnome,robot,alien}.ts`, toy figures like the heroes, never minifigures): the elder by the heart, three grown-ups strolling between the doors, a little one playing. They wave at the party; they dance to music; during a wave they hide indoors.
- **Safe like home**: within 30 of the centre no monster appears (except the task's wave) and hearts come back as fast as at home.

## Tales
Always one tale under way: a little story of 1–4 steps made of things the game already has (cards `src/content/tales.ts`, tested; run by `src/systems/tales.ts`; models `src/assets/tales.ts`). Requesters are Пупся, the races and Бабуся, never the children (they are the players).
- **Prologue «Пупсині рибки»** (once per story, a few seconds after the heroes wake; old saves skip it): Пупся's fish were stolen in the night → two fish in the yard (7–13 from home), each turning over a sparkle column, a mark on the map, the nearest with the rim arrow (teaches running) → the third is carried by Зомбік ~20 away, a fish in its hand, its mark followed live (teaches the bow); beaten, it drops the fish → «put a tent by the house», the kit box pulses (teaches the kit; the old 90-second build tip is not said) → the elves' village gets a mark with the rim arrow (the first long walk).
- **How a tale comes**: Бабуся's by a **stork** carrying the letter, the same way as a plane; a race's tale by **paper plane** — when all is calm (no line playing, no monster near unless at home, no boss, album closed) a paper dart with the race's stamp flies in from the way of its village and lands in front of the hero; touched (or after 15 s) it pops: «Ой, літачок! Нам лист!», then the race's voice asks. **The postbox**: a letter due while the party is within 30 of home flies into the postbox instead (see «Home life»); far away, once the portal is there, it goes straight into the postbox and waits — a plane mark with the rim arrow at home on the map, Пупся says so once — and unopened by the next morning or after 5 minutes it flies to the party itself; without the portal it flies to the hero as before. One letter waits at a time. Пупся's tale by **dream** — after a night's sleep (home, tent, guest house) «Мені наснилося…» with the step's picture over her head for a moment; in the evening, when the next is a dream, she asks to go to bed. A day passed without sleep: the next letter comes first, the dream keeps its place.
- **Order**: one at a time, no choosing; the next comes the day after the last one is done. Льоша's own tales (`lyosha: true`) come first («Цю казку вигадав Льоша!»). A tale needing a building the child hasn't got waits until there is one.
- **Steps**: gather N things (a spread around a place), catch a thief, put up a building near a place, reach a place (home, a village), music for N s, a defence wave, a night's sleep, light a lighthouse. Each step's ask is said when it begins, on a tap on the tale piece, and as a reminder: when a step stands still ~75 s, Пупся's next thought is its ask (otherwise now and then the kit box's task; the box also names its next task a few seconds after each reward).
- **The end**: the asker thanks, a fanfare, coins, and the tale's **page in the book of tales**: the album gets a row of its own, a page per tale (dark until done); a tap retells the tale in one sentence; Льоша's pages glow gold.
- **Artifacts** (the owner's call, 2026-10-30): one rare thing per tale or castle, found at the end (with the tale's thanks, or in the castle's chest): golden fireworks over the party, then its line. While the party has it a lasting power is on (hearts back fast, fast running, monsters near dancing or waiting, no monsters near at night). The album shows the artifacts on a row of their own after the book of tales, **the treasures** («скарби»), each with a gold rim; dark until found.
- **Companions**: a tale, a castle or a reward may bring a friend who walks with the party (behind it, beside the robot helper's place), cannot be hurt, and does one trick — shoots monsters like the robot helper, gives a hurt hero a heart now and then, or keeps monsters away at night. Not playable: more playable heroes are a later, separate task (the owner's call, 2026-10-30).
- **Льоша's own** monsters, items and artifacts (`lyosha: true`) have the gold rim on their album stickers too.
- The first set (in order): Пупсині рибки; Пупсин скарб (three pieces of a map → a chest far out that opens and bursts coins); дискотека для ельфів (a boombox → music); нічна варта (a wave at home → a night's sleep); гноми кличуть; клубочки (five balls of yarn around the hero → home); маяк для гномів (needs a power building); ліхтарі роботів (needs a lamp); роботи кличуть; інопланетяни кличуть.
- A new tale (stage 3, the skill `.claude/skills/new-tale`): a card plus its lines; new things to gather are a model in `src/assets/tales.ts` and a baked picture.

## Guide
- «?» in the pause menu opens a plate of picture cards built from the HUD pieces and game renders: run (joystick), shoot (bow + monster), jump (spring → hero up in the air), lightning (half-lit charge ring), buildings (kit box → tent), heal (apple + heart + house), map (minimap with the house and the boss arrow), tales (paper plane → the tale piece → the book), portal (house → arch), sleep (moon + tent), power (windmill, pole, lamp), lighthouse (windmill → lit lighthouse), disco (big speaker, dance floor, DJ console), defence (tesla tower, traffic light), village (elder → the thing to gather → the garland), food (picnic → apple → heart), power station (station → lamp), and the boss as a 3-step strip: three lanterns → cracked armour → arrow into the open glowing mouth. A tap on a card plays its voiced line (guide_*) and the picture hops. Coral ✕ or a tap outside closes; the game stays paused.
- Captions are short and for parents only; the pictures and the voice explain.
- The first time a save enters the boss's wasteland, after its roar the game pauses on the boss card alone (with guide_boss) and a ▶; any tap continues. Remembered in save.said as guide_boss.

## Contents
«Зміст гри» (the owner's call, 2026-10-31): everything the game has, for the parents, in Ukrainian text with pictures (`src/content/contents.ts`, tested; `src/ui/contents.ts`). Opened from the pause menu, over it; coral ✕ or a tap outside goes back to the menu.
- A studded plate: the title and a tile per section on top (a tap scrolls there), below a list that scrolls with a finger — the only scrolling in the game.
- Sections: what is waiting next (a few rows, each jumping to its section), the kit box (its task and every reward in order), buildings, villages, castles, tales, things, monsters, lighthouses and both giants (the one up now, the other as what may come after a win, each with its own wins); treasures and friends appear once such cards exist.
- A row is a flat cream card with even corners (no glow, no bevel): the thing's picture, its name, one sentence of what it does (`about` on its card), and where it stands — had (✓, how many, the upgrade level as gold studs), under way (a gold rim, «4 з 10»), or still ahead (the picture faded, the words a little muted but fully readable, how to get it). Villages and castles not yet found say the way: the side of the world and how far from home.
- A tap anywhere on a card, had or not, hops its picture and plays its voiced line (a blue speaker at the card's right shows it speaks; a row of «Що чекає далі» also has an arrow to its section) (the building's first line, the item's, the monster's, the tale's opening or retelling, the guide's). No new voice.
- Everything comes from the cards and the save, so a new card shows up by itself.

## Voice frequency
- **Every line is voiced before it ships** (the owner's call, 2026-10-30): the deploy runs the content check with `STRICT_VOICE=1`, so a line without its mp3 stops publishing. Lines are shown to the owner first and voiced only after the yes.
- Пупся is chatty on purpose (the owner's call): she keeps the child company. Rules in `src/systems/pacing.ts` (tested) only stop her talking over herself: a thought every ~2–3 minutes, waiting for any playing line to end; the night tip every night.
- Boss fight: the stone hint whenever arrows bounce off the armour (at most every 25 s); the mouth hint after the armour breaks and when shots hit the closed mouth (at most every 35 s); the stomp warning once per session; one line per lantern. A boss card that popped up counts as the hint it explained.

## Performance (no freezes)
A frame that stalls is felt as the game breaking. The rules, measured by `npm run perf` (the real start, then a fixed route: a walk, every village, the portal home, the map; it lists freezes, the worst frame and every shader built mid-play):
- **No light that comes and goes.** A change in the number of real lights rebuilds every material. Only the sun, the sky, the house lamp and the campfire are lights, all there from the start; everything else glows with a halo and a pool on the ground (portals, lanterns, lamps).
- **Prepared before shown.** A new object whose shaders were never built is built in the background and stays hidden until ready, a split second late instead of a frozen frame (`src/render/shaderGate.ts`). While the title is up, the other quality look is prepared too.
- **The look changes only at quiet moments.** The quality control steps down at once when frames are slow, but steps up only behind a pause, the big map or a dark fade (`src/core/quality.ts`). The rendered picture is capped at 3.6 million pixels.
- **Built once, kept.** Villages, lighthouses and bridges out of range are put aside, not thrown away (the nearest 10 lighthouses and 20 bridges; farther ones are let go); a village's first build is spread over frames, a piece a frame.
- **Budgets per frame.** New land, its meshes, coins and dressing are made a step at a time within a few milliseconds a frame (`src/world/World.ts`).
- `?stats=1` shows the worst frame of the last 5 s, freezes (over 100 ms), the shader count, and on a second row how many monsters are alive, how many of them are hidden, and what spawning has run into so far.
